# ****Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ Whangarei.****

We are proud to be your local outdoor power equipment solutions provider.For over 30 years we have called Northland home and take pride in supporting our local customers – homeowners, contractors,farmers, businesses & schools.  
  
We provide a great range of products and services: outdoor power and gardening equipment and accessories as well as a service and repair centre. Tiny or huge we have the equipment to Love your Land. We offer the full range of Stihl products and:

[BOOK A SERVICE]

We offer the full range of Stihl products including:

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   Garden tools
*   Service and repair reputable brands of outdoor power equipment.
*   Protective and work wear
*   Trade accounts for local businesses
*   Free expert technical advice and support

When you bring in your tools to be serviced, we also offer a range of battery-powered courtesy equipment that you can take home and try for yourself.  
  
We are proud to be part of the STIHL family who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in Northland and look forward to meeting you soon.We are open seven days for your convenience

# Service

## Regular servicing of your outdoor power equipment will safeguard what you value the most in our STIHL products – longevity and performance.

We service & fix most brands & have a trained team to help you with your servicing and repair needs. With our factory-trained and certified technicians, your gear will be in the best hands possible.  
  
We offer prompt and effective service not only for your STIHL products, but for other products too using genuine parts.  
  
We have a fully equipped service workshop with the latest diagnostic tools to keep your machine running as it should as well as expert advice to ensure you get the best out of your equipment.
