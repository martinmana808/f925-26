## Self-Service Your STIHL Tools with Our Comprehensive Kits

Ensure the longevity and optimal performance of your STIHL tools with our easy-to-use service kits at Stihl Shop Napier. Designed for a variety of STIHL equipment, including chainsaws, line trimmers, blowers, hedge trimmers, and brushcutters, these kits empower you to perform regular maintenance at home. Complete with online video instructions, our service kits provide all the essentials needed to keep your tools running smoothly, helping you take control of upkeep and extend the life of your investments.

### [service kits]

[View models]

## How to Use STIHL Service Kits

#### Step-by-Step Guidance

Follow our clear instructional videos that provide detailed, easy-to-follow procedures tailored to each type of tool. These resources are designed to help you confidently perform maintenance tasks without any guesswork.

#### Complete Kit Contents

Our kits include all necessary components, such as filters, spark plugs, and the appropriate lubricants, to ensure comprehensive maintenance. This one-stop solution streamlines the servicing process, making it efficient and straightforward.

#### Support and Advice

Should you need further assistance, Stihl Shop Napier is here to help. Our team is always ready to provide expert advice and support, ensuring you get the most out of your service kits and STIHL equipment. This content not only educates customers on the importance of regular tool maintenance but also promotes the self-sufficiency provided by STIHL service kits, making Stihl Shop Napier the preferred choice for purchasing and learning about tool care.

# About stihl shop Napier

The Stihl Shop Napier is proudly owned and operated by Marty and Debbie Leach.

Our mission is to provide the best quality Outdoor Power Equipment through sales, service and friendly advice to the Hawkes Bay community and beyond.

### Expert product knowledge

### Expert servicing knowledge

We are also Napier’s Specialist Weber BBQ dealership, stocking and supporting this premium brand with knowledge and stories of how to create memorable BBQ cooking results. We look forward to hearing your BBQ adventures and sharing stories.

Situated in Thackeray St (next to Rebel Sport), we have 450m² of OPE & BBQ’s on display in a modern, welcoming showroom complimented with a thriving workshop to support your needs.

To ensure our customers’ satisfaction Stihl Shop Napier only employ staff who support our policies 100%. Our staff are qualified technicians across the entire Stihl range. We all attend regular customer service training sessions and consistently achieve top ratings with our customer care surveys.

Shopping in the best environment – owning and using the best brands, sold and serviced by the best people – offers you the best opportunity for a successful solution no matter what your application.
