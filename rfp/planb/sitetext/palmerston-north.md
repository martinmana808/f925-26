# SERVICE

At STIHL SHOP Palmerston North, we’re all about keeping your outdoor power equipment running like it should. Regular servicing helps protect what matters most – reliability, performance, and getting the longest life out of your machine.  
  
Our experienced local team services and repairs for both STIHL and non-STIHL products. With factory-trained certified technicians in our workshop, you can feel confident your gear is in good hands and getting the care it deserves.  
  
We offer prompt, dependable servicing using genuine parts, so your equipment performs properly and stays job-ready. Our workshop is fully equipped with modern diagnostic tools, and we’re always happy to share practical advice to help you get the best out of your machine – whether it’s for work, a lifestyle block, or home use.  
  
Call our team on [**06 358 6275**] today to book your service!
