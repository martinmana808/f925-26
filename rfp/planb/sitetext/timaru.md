## **Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ Timaru.**

We are proud to be your local outdoor power equipment solutions provider. We have lived in the Timaru District for many years and take pride in supporting our local customers –home owners,contractors & businesses & schools.  
  
We provide a great range of products and services: outdoor power and gardening equipment and accessories as well as a service and repair centre. Tiny or huge we have the equipment to Love your Land. We offer the full range of Stihl products and,

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   Garden tools
*   Service and repair all makes of outdoor power equipment.
*   Protective and work wear
*   Trade accounts for local businesses
*   Free expert technical advice and support

When you bring in your tools to be serviced, we also offer a range of battery-powered courtesy equipment that you can take home and try for yourself.

We are proud to be part of the STIHL family who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in South Canterbury areas and look forward to meeting you soon.We are open 6 days for your convenience

## Stihl Shop Timaru

Our product always comes fully assembled and is well laid out in a bright clean store, so you really get a great sense of the quality on offer.

Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands – you can rest assured you’ll be getting sound advice.

Ask about our range of battery-powered equipment including lawnmowers, hedge cutters, trimmers, blower vacs & chainsaws which are extremely popular with homeowners.

Our fully equipped workshop and trained technicians give us the ability to stand firmly behind every product we sell. You will receive expert service and we are committed to making your experience the best it can be, right from pre-delivery through to after-sales service.

## Regular servicing of your outdoor power equipment will safeguard what you value the most in our STIHL products – longevity and performance.

We offer prompt and effective service not only for your STIHL products, but for other products too using genuine parts.
