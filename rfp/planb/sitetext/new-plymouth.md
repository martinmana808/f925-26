# **Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ New Plymouth & Stratford.**

Local owners Hamish & Kate Scott are here to provide you with only the best brands of outdoor power equipment, and therefore the best possible solution to fit your needs.

Our product always comes fully assembled and is well laid out in a bright clean store, so you really get a great sense of the quality on offer. Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands – you can rest assured you’ll be getting sound advice.

However, if you’re still not certain what product is for you, ask about our range of battery-powered demonstration equipment that you can take home and try for yourself.

Our fully equipped workshop and trained technicians give us the ability to stand firmly behind every product we sell. You will receive expert service and we are committed to making your experience the best it can be, right from pre-delivery through to after-sales service.

With STIHL™ setting the standard in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in Taranaki and surrounding areas and look forward to meeting you soon.

**So come on in and deal directly with people who actually use the gear.**

# Service

We offer a prompt and effective service not only for your Stihl outdoor products but for other brands.

We are now authorised service agents for **Walker Ride On Mowers** as well as **Stihl, Hansa, Masport, Hustler, Cub Cadet, Honda, Rover, Stiga, Lawnmaster, Briggs & Stratton, Victa & Morrison** outdoor power equipment.

## Your gear - our certified technicians!

Regular servicing of your outdoor power equipment will safeguard what you value the most in our STIHL products – longevity and performance.

We service and repair all STIHL gear as well as all other major brands. With our factory-trained and certified technicians, your gear will be in the best hands possible.

We offer prompt and effective service using genuine parts.
