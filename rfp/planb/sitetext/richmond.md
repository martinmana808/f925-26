## STIHL Shop Richmond

STIHL SHOP Richmond has built a strong reputation as a one-stop destination for outdoor power equipment. The team prides itself on delivering outstanding sales and service, backed by knowledgeable advice from experienced professionals. By stocking only trusted, premium brands, they ensure customers receive reliable, high-quality equipment every time.

As long-time users of the equipment they sell, the team confidently stands behind every product in store. This means you’re not only purchasing high-performing outdoor equipment—you’re also getting expert guidance from people who truly understand the tools they supply.

STIHL SHOP Richmond carries a wide range of leading outdoor power equipment, including STIHL tools, Masport mowers, Walker ride-on mowers, Hansa chippers, Cub Cadet mowers, Hustler mowers, and more.

## Our Services

The experienced service team at STIHL SHOP Richmond has been working with outdoor power equipment for many years and is always ready to offer friendly, expert advice on servicing and repairs.

Their fully equipped workshop provides specialised after-sales service for all brands, makes, and models of outdoor power equipment.

From lawn mowers, chainsaws, and brush cutters to chippers, generators, hedge trimmers, and weedeaters, STIHL SHOP Richmond services a wide range of equipment. Using the latest diagnostic tools, the team can quickly and accurately identify problems—saving you both time and money.

They also offer a comprehensive sharpening service for chainsaws, secateurs, hedge clippers, mowers, and mulchers. Get in touch with the friendly team today to discuss your servicing needs.

## STIHL Shop Marine

**STIHL SHOP Marine** is an authorised **Mercury** and **Suzuki Marine** Service and Repower Centre.

Our team supports local boat owners with everything they need — from initial pre-purchase inspections through to ongoing servicing and maintenance for all brands of boats and outboard motors. We can also help you into a new boat, offering trusted brands such as **Surtees** and **Kiwi Kraft**, ensuring you’re ready to get out on the water with confidence.

## Our Services

Keeping your boat, outboard, and trailer in top condition is essential for safety and reliability. The team at STIHL SHOP Marine provides regular servicing to keep everything running smoothly.

With support from trusted local businesses, STIHL SHOP Marine can manage a wide range of marine services in one place, including WOFs, servicing, wiring and electronic installations, boat repairs, trailer repairs, repowers, and complete refurbishments.

The team offers honest, friendly advice and will clearly explain any work that may be needed, along with estimated costs, so there are no surprises.

Visit the STIHL SHOP Marine team on Gladstone Road, just behind the STIHL SHOP retail store, and they will help ensure your boat is ready to perform at its best. Known for quality workmanship and reliable parts and accessories, their focus is always on great results and customer satisfaction—so you can get back to fishing, water-skiing, and enjoying time out on the water with confidence.
