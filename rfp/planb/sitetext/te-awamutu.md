## STIHL SHOP Te Awamutu is one of Waikato's Leading outdoor power equipment retailers and service workshops.

We specialise in top quality ride on mowers, outdoor power equipment and home heating. We stock the best and most respected brands including STIHL, Walker, Masport, Honda Power Equipment, Hansa and Firenzo. Our staff have extensive knowledge and are ready to assist you in making the right decision when buying outdoor power equipment to meet both your requirements and your budget.

Our friendly efficient service is second to none and we have a diverse range of mechanical and engineering expertise to meet your needs.

Matt and Sharlene Hird along with the team offer full retail sales & service, having skilled technicians operating in a fully equipped workshop.

## SERVICE

STIHL SHOP Te Awamutu’s service technicians are qualified to service most leading brand outdoor power equipment.

Ongoing care and maintenance of outdoor power equipment is critical to ensure it continues to deliver top results for years to come. At STIHL SHOP Te Awamutu we service what we sell – and all other major brands – whether it be power, electric or battery outdoor power equipment.

Your machine is guaranteed to be well taken care of with our factory trained technicians who are experts in what they do.

Notably,STIHL SHOP Te Awamutu specialises in lawn more repair, lawn mower service, chainsaw repair, and chainsaw service – as well as repair and servicing of other power tools.
