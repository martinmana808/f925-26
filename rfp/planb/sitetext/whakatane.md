# Welcome to STIHL SHOP Whakatane

STIHL SHOP is a national chain of independant, locally owned specialist outdoor power equipment dealers. At STIHL SHOP we know outdoor power equipment inside and out.

Long established STIHL SHOP Whakatane is the local agent for STIHL products and we are the people to see for any of your outdoor power equipment.

We offer great choices, with a range of prices, brands and equipment to suit any needs.

We support everything we sell with our fully equipped workshop and highly skilled technical staff.

At STIHL SHOP our friendly and approachable staff can help you with any questions you may have.

After sales service is also very important to us. Our Industry trained service technicians can efficiently identify what is required,saving you time and money.

We guarantee to carry out any necessary repairs to the highest standard.

Whether you are a weekend warrior or a professional, STIHL SHOP has the right advice and right equipment for the job.

**We only sell products built to last and do the job.**

**Get the best tool for the job and do it right the first time.**

## Service & Repairs

[Home]/Service & Repairs

**STIHL SHOP Whakatane has a fully equipped service workshop with the latest in diagnostic equipment to help identify problems accurately and efficiently, saving you time and money.**

**We service what we sell together with any other brands of outdoor power equipment, regardless of Brand.**

**We service and sell an extensive range of Ride-on mowers**, **lawnmowers,chainsaws, line-trimmers brushcutters, cut off saws, spray equipment…in fact just about anything with a small engine and can replace parts on all our products if a problem should arise.**

**We pride ourselves on our after sales service and every new machine sold comes fully assembled and is pre-run and ready to go, with in-store training from qualified experts, ensuring trouble free operation.**

**StihlShop Whakatane also offer key cutting and chain sharpening as part of their extensive range of services.**
