## ****WELCOME TO STIHL SHOP™ HOWICK & HIRE****

We’re Garth and Tessa Deenik — proud owners of STIHL SHOP™ Howick and your trusted local experts in outdoor power equipment. As long-time East Auckland residents, we take great pride in supporting our local community — from homeowners and contractors to schools and businesses.  
  
We offer a comprehensive range of **products and services** to help you get the job done right:

*    Blower, Line trimmers, Hedge trimmer, Chippers and Chainsaws 
*    Lawnmowers, ride-on mowers, generators, and water pumps 
*    Wet & Forget and Ecomist Stockists 
*    Garden & General waste bags 
*    BBQ’s and Accessories and Charcoal 
*    Service and repairs for all makes & models 
*    Key cutting 

Whether your project is big or small, we have the equipment and expertise to help you **Love Your Land.**

When you bring in your tools to be serviced, we also offer a range of battery-powered courtesy equipment that you can take home and try for yourself.

****HIRE SHOP –We now HIRE outdoor power & garden equipment.We have a range of chainsaws, hedge trimmers, line trimmers, lawnmowers, water blasters, shredders and more for HIRE.****

**It is easy and cost effective so give us a ring to book – 09 5328443**

We are proud to be part of the STIHL family who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in Howick and Eastern areas and look forward to meeting you soon.We are open 7 days for your convenience
