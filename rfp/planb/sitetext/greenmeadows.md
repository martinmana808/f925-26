# ****WELCOME TO STIHL SHOP Greenmeadows****, Napier.

Stihl Shop Greenmeadows in Napier is a locally owned, family run business proudly supporting Napier and the wider Hawkes Bay region.  
  
We specialise in outdoor power equipment, heating solutions and mobility scooters, offering expert advice, quality products and reliable servicing you can trust.  
  
Whether you’re a homeowner, lifestyle block owner or commercial operator, our experienced team is here to help you find the right solution for the job – and keep it running smoothly

[VIEW PROMOTIONS]

[BOOK A SERVICE]

Our service and repair centre has fully trained technicians with spare parts stocks which makes our store a complete go-to for your needs.  
  
Our Products and Services include:

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   All Outdoor Power Equipment
*   Garden tools
*   Woodfires & home heating solutions
*   Mobility scooters
*   Service and repair of reputable brands of outdoor power equipment
*   Protective and work wear
*   Expert technical advice and support

We are proud to be licensed dealers here in Napier for STIHL who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for 100 years.

Our products always come fully assembled and are on display in our bright clean store here in Napier.  
  
Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands. From our sales team to our technicians, you can rest assured you’ll be getting great advice for the tools to suit your needs.

Battery-powered equipment including lawnmowers, hedge cutters, trimmers, blower vacs, pressure cleaners, chainsaws & more are extremely proficient, but if you need convincing, we do have some demonstration tools to try.  
  
Our workshop is fully equipped with diagnostic and specialist STIHL tools and our experienced & trained technicians ably provide service and repairs for every product we sell.   
  
You will receive expert service and we are committed to making your experience the best it can be, right from pre-delivery through to after-sales service.
