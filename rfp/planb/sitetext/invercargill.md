# Welcome to STIHL SHOP Invercargill

STIHL SHOP Invercargill is a family owned and operated business that has been in Invercargill since 1985. We sell and service major brands including STIHL, Honda, Masport, Walker, Ferris, Cub Cadet, Stiga, Castel Garden, Rover, MTD, Lawnmaster, Victa, Testarossa, Silky saws, Wolf Garden, Kiwi Care, Precision and Weber BBQ.

For quality tools and brands you can trust, STIHL SHOP Invercargill has the knowledge and advice to match you with the right tool for the job. We sell products that we believe outlast and outperform the rest. We have hedge trimmers, line trimmers, water blasters, blowers and shredders and more.

At STIHL SHOP Invercargill, we service what we sell. With large spare part inventory on site, whether its power, electric or battery outdoor power equipment. From chainsaws to ride-on mowers; with our workshop, we can service and repair your tools, so you can get the job done.

# About STIHL SHOP Invercargill

[]

[]

[]

[]

[]

[]

STIHL SHOP Invercargill is a family owned and operated business that began in Invercargill in 1985. It has been under the STIHL SHOP brand since 2004 and sells and services a wide range of products for major brands including STIHL, Weber, Masport, Honda, Testarossa and Morrison.

As experts in their field and with an extensive range of tools, Don, Shirley, Gill, Grace, and their staff aim to match the customer to the right tool for the job every time. They also offer parts, accessories and the safety equipment and when it comes to maintaining your lawn, Stihl Shop Invercargill have you covered with their wide range of walk behind and ride-on lawn mowers, including Masport, Lawnmaster, Victa, Walker, Castel Garden, MTD, Rover, Cub Cadet and Countax.

Staff at STIHL SHOP Invercargill are qualified small engine service technicians and STIHL factory qualified, giving customers solid technical advice on any product purchase. STIHL SHOP Invercargill also has a fully equipped workshop which can service a wide range of products including petrol, electrical and an increasing range of cordless battery powered equipment.

Shirley says their store stocks quality brands and their staff have excellent knowledge of the equipment they sell. ‘When you buy from STIHL SHOP Invercargill we ensure the tool is started, tested and ready to go and will also be accompanied by a warranty and expert advice.’

They also sell Honda engines, pumps and generators, Hansa chippers and shredders, log splitters, Briggs and Stratton replacement engines and parts and a large range of STIHL and Timbersport t-shirts, jackets, caps, toys and more. STIHL SHOP Invercargill are an official Weber Specialist and authorised dealer. To see their extensive range, visit STIHL SHOP Invercargill at 120 Tay Street, Invercargill.
