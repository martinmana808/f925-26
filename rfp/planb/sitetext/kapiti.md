# Garden Machinery Servicing & Repair

#### The shop has qualified technicians with over 30 years experience so you can be confident the job will be completed to the highest standard first time.

Stihl Shop Kapiti provide a repair service for all types of contractors, both central and local government, golf clubs, retirement villages, bowling clubs, home gardeners, and anyone using equipment with a small engine.

We service and repair most makes of garden machinery and small engines including:

*   Lawn Mowers and Ride-on Mowers
*   Chainsaws and Hedge Trimmers
*   Weedeaters, Brushcutters and Edgers
*   Blower Vacs
*   Mulchers and Log Splitter
*   Golf Carts, Turf Rollers, Log Conveyors and Pest Smokers
