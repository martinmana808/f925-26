## Welcome to STIHL Shop Greymouth

Proudly locally owned and operated, STIHL Shop Greymouth is your go-to destination for quality outdoor power tools, Woodsman log burners, and expert advice. Whether you're after a reliable chainsaw, a ride-on mower, or a multi fuel fire, we’ve got the right solution for your home, lifestyle block, or professional needs. We proudly stock Woodsman’s NZ-made Multifuel fires and Ultra Low Emission burners, known for their efficiency, easy operation, and sleek design. Our knowledgeable team can help you choose the perfect fire and provide in-store installation quotes. We also offer servicing and repairs for all major brands of outdoor power equipment in our fully equipped workshop, handled by trusted technicians. Drop in and experience top-notch service, premium gear, and genuine know-how – all backed by the STIHL and Woodsman names.

# Service & Repairs

STIHL tools are built to last and with good maintenance you can extend the life even further and ensure good performance and safe operation.

​

At STIHL Shop Greymouth, we take pride in offering ongoing technical support to keep your equipment performing at its best. Whether you’ve purchased your tools from us in-store, online, or elsewhere—we’re here to help.

​

Our experienced, factory-trained STIHL certified technicians are equipped to service and repair not only the STIHL range but all major brands of outdoor power equipment. Whether its two-stroke, four-stroke, electric or battery, we have the expertise and tools to keep your machine running smoothly.

​

Bring your tools in with confidence—STIHL Shop Greymouth has you covered.
