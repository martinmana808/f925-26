About[jamiebell]2020-05-12T12:30:17+12:00

### **Stihl Shop Matamata has been servicing the Matamata, Piako and wider southern-Waikato region for over 30 years. We have the extensive range of equipment for all things power tools and pride ourselves on delivering service that is second to none.**

### **Brett and Shelly are the proud new owners of Stihl Shop Matamata.**

Both Shelly and Brett have been living on a lifestyle block for the past 13 years, busy bringing up two daughters, tending to the animals and grooming the ever-growing formal gardens.

Shelly has been in corporate accounting and financial positions for the past 17 years and more recently moved to a large Engineering business as Finance Manager.Brett has spent the last 11 years working for a large National Brewer. 8 of the past 11 years as a Field Sales Manager. The opportunity to purchase a well-established small business in a fantastic small town was too good an opportunity to pass up. As very keen fire-wooder and outdoorsman,Brett is right in his element selling the machinery he knows well and is genuinely passionate about.

> “We are both extremely motivated to offer the community a full range of domestic and commercial Stihl product, complemented by a large range of Hustler and Cub Cadet ride on mower options. We back all of our sales with a highly qualified and competent ‘Stihl Platinum qualified technician’-Simon Gill”

Our Mission is to offer 100% of our Service to 100% of the people 100% of time!

### **Simon Gill**

Stihl Shop Matamata is proud to have a fully qualified Platinum Certified Stihl Technician in the business.

Simon has been a Mechanic of varying equipment for the past 25 years across a range of small and large engines. Industry qualifications include

*   Platinum Certified Stihl Technician
*   Qualified Motor Cycle Engineer
*   Certified papers as a Diesel Technician

Aside from boasting an impressive collection of over 40 Stihl Chainsaws, Simon enjoys competitive Hotsaw Racing, custom Chain fabrication, and taking on any challenging technical or mechanical situations that are presented.

Simon’s genuine passion for excellence and customer relations adds a dynamic advantage to Simons offering.
