## Welcome to STIHL Shop Ferrymead

Proudly locally owned and operated, STIHL Shop Ferrymead is your go-to destination for quality outdoor power tools, Woodsman log burners, and expert advice. Whether you're after a reliable chainsaw, a ride-on mower, or a clean-air compliant wood fire, we’ve got the right solution for your home, lifestyle block, or professional needs. We proudly stock Woodsman’s NZ-made ultra-low emission burners, known for their efficiency, easy operation, and sleek design. Our knowledgeable team can help you choose the perfect fire and provide in-store installation quotes. We also offer servicing and repairs for all major brands of outdoor power equipment in our fully equipped workshop, handled by trusted technicians. Drop in and experience top-notch service, premium gear, and genuine know-how – all backed by the STIHL and Woodsman names.

# Service & Repairs

STIHL tools are built to last and with good maintenance you can extend the life even further and ensure good performance and safe operation.

​

## About Us

STIHL Shop Ferrymead is proudly owned and operated by Bill and Shelley Caines – long-time locals with a passion for quality gear and genuine service. As directors, Bill and Shelley bring their extensive experience from the West Coast, where they’ve successfully run STIHL stores for over four years. Their son Michael now manages the Ferrymead store, continuing the family’s hands-on, customer-first approach.​

​

We’re your local team for trusted outdoor power equipment, expert advice, and full servicing – whether you're maintaining a lifestyle block, tidying up around the home, or getting set for winter with a new fire.​

​

We stock the full STIHL range, as well as Woodsman, Masport, Hansa, and other top brands. Plus, with an on-site workshop, we’ll keep your gear running at its best year-round.

​

Come and see us at STIHL Shop Ferrymead – where local knowledge meets world-class equipment.
