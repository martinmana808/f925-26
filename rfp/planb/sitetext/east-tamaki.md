## **Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ East Tamaki.**

Hamish Cook and his team are here to provide you with only the best brands of outdoor power equipment, and therefore the best possible solution to fit your needs.  
  
Our product always comes fully assembled and is well laid out in a bright clean store, so you really get a great sense of the quality on offer. Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands – you can rest assured you’ll be getting sound advice.  
  
Ask about our range of battery-powered equipment including lawnmowers, hedge cutters, trimmers, blower vacs & chainsaws which are extremely popular with homeowners.  
  
Our fully equipped workshop and trained technicians give us the ability to stand firmly behind every product we sell. You will receive expert service and we are committed to making your experience the best it can be, right from pre-delivery through to after-sales service.  
  
With STIHL™ setting the standard in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in South & East Auckland and surrounding areas and look forward to meeting you soon.

## Sthil Shop East Tamaki

We are local and are here to help with your home maintenance and construction needs. We’ve got lawnmowers, hedge cutters, trimmers, blower vacs, chippers, ride on mowers, high pressure cleaners, chainsaws, sprayers, misters, brushcutters, safety equipment, lubricants, line trimmers, generators, pumps, clearing saws, outdoor power equipment and more!
