# ****Welcome to STIHL SHOP® Browns Bay.****

STIHL SHOP Browns Bay is your locally owned outdoor power equipment retailer, providing solutions for homeowners, contractors, farmers, businesses & schools in the North Shore and Rodney region.  
  
We provide a great range of outdoor power tools, gardening equipment products plus accessories.

[VIEW PROMOTIONS]

[BOOK A SERVICE]

We offer the full range of Stihl products including:

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   Garden tools
*   Service and repair most makes of outdoor power equipment.
*   Protective and work wear
*   Trade accounts for local businesses
*   Free expert technical advice and support

We are proud to be part of the STIHL family who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for over 80 years, we are excited to represent this iconic brand in Browns Bay and look forward to meeting you soon.

## **We are open SEVEN days for your convenience so come on in and deal directly with the folks who actually use the gear.**

Our products always come fully assembled and are on display in a bright clean store, so you can assess the quality on offer.  
  
Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands. From our sales team to our technicians, you can rest assured you’ll be getting great advice for the tools to suit your needs.

Battery-powered equipment including lawnmowers, hedge cutters, trimmers, blower vacs, pressure cleaners, chainsaws & more are extremely proficient, but if you need convincing, talk to us and we will see how we can arrange a trial.  
  
Our workshop is fully equipped with diagnostic and specialist STIHL tools and our experienced & trained technicians ably provide service and repairs for every product we sell.   
  
You will receive expert service and we are committed to making your experience the best it can be, right from pre-delivery through to after-sales service.

# Service

## Regular servicing of your outdoor power equipment will safeguard what you value the most in our STIHL products – longevity and performance.

We service & fix most brands & have a trained team to help you with your servicing and repair needs. With our factory-trained and certified technicians, your gear will be in the best hands possible.  
  
We offer prompt and effective service not only for your STIHL products, but for other products too using genuine parts.  
  
We have a fully equipped service workshop with the latest diagnostic tools to keep your machine running as it should as well as expert advice to ensure you get the best out of your equipment.
