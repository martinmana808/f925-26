# Welcome to STIHL SHOP Winton

STIHL SHOP Winton in central Southland is your locally owned and operated Outdoor Power Equipment specialist since 1994, offering premium brands you can trust such as STIHL, Honda, Masport, LawnMaster, Cub Cadet, Walker, Hustler, Stiga, Solo, Silky and Hansa.

You'll find an extensive range of quality ride on mowers, lawnmowers, chainsaws, waterblasters, trimmers, blowers, shredders, log splitters, pumps and generators to suit Southland conditions and customers from home domestic users through to farming, forestry, and commercial users. We stock products that we believe outlast and outperform the rest, complimented by an extensive range of accessories, safety equipment and spare parts.

​

We are also Southland's proud stockists of Avanti, Scott, Merida and Silverback bicycles, including electric bikes and cycle accessories.

​

Fraser, Sarah and the team at STIHL SHOP Winton have the knowledge and advice to match you with the right tool for the job.

# About STIHL SHOP Winton

Located in the heart of central Southland, STIHL SHOP Winton is your locally owned and operated outdoor power equipment and cycle specialist.

STIHL SHOP Winton is a family owned business, operating for 30 years and is now owned by Sarah and Fraser Guise. Throughout the 30 years, we have ensured a wide range of products from premium brands are available to suit the Southland customers and conditions. These include Stihl petrol and battery powered garden tools and robotic mowers; Cub Cadet, Rover, Hustler, Walker and Stiga ride-on mowers; Masport, Lawnmaster and Honda lawnmowers; Hansa and Stevens chippers and Honda pumps and generators; all to suit home, farming, forestry and commercial users.

​

This equipment is complimented by an extensive range of accessories, safety equipment and spare parts.

A full range of reliable Avanti, Scott, Merida cycles, including electric bikes and MGP scooters are in-store to suit the whole family.

In our modern workshop our skilled technicians not only service and repair all makes and models of outdoor power equipment but also service and repair all brands of cycles, including servicing of electric bikes using the latest diagnostic software.

All new products purchased in-store are fully assembled, fuelled and ready to use and include a complimentary first service.  
  
 At STIHL SHOP Winton our friendly and helpful staff will ensure you get the expert advice and quality service you need.  
  
 We look forward to seeing you soon! In the meantime, take a virtual tour of our showroom and don't hesitate to get in touch if you have any queries.

# Services and Repairs

At STIHL SHOP Winton, we not only service and repair the machines we sell instore and online - we proudly look after all other major brands of Outdoor Power Equipment.

Whether it's a 2 stroke, 4 stroke, electric or battery powered machine, our trained technicians have the know how to keep your equipment in top condition. STIHL SHOP Winton has a fully equipped workshop with the latest diagnostic tools to keep your machine running as it should.

​

We also service and repair all brands of push bikes including servicing of e-bikes using the latest diagnostic software.

​

We are proud stockists of genuine STIHL, Walker, Honda, Briggs & Stratton, Masport, Cub Cadet, Stiga, Hansa, Scott, Avanti and Merida spare parts.

​

If you have difficultly getting your lawnmower to us for servicing, we offer a pick up and delivery service.
