## **Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ Feilding.**

Proud local owners, Richard Baker and Dan Burr, are your local outdoor power equipment solutions provider with only the best brands to fit your needs. Having lived in the mighty Manawatu District for many years we take pride in supporting our local customers – homeowners, contractors,farmers, businesses & schools.  
  
We provide a great range of products and services: outdoor power and gardening equipment and accessories as well as a service and repair centre. Tiny or huge we have the equipment to Love your Land. We offer the full range of Stihl products and,

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   Garden tools
*   Service and repair all makes of outdoor power equipment.
*   Protective and work wear
*   Trade accounts for local businesses
*   Free expert technical advice and support

Our product always comes fully assembled in a well laid out, bright clean store. Our team of friendly staff have practical experience with our core brands so you can rest assured you’ll be getting sound professional advice.
