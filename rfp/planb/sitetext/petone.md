# **Your Award-Winning One-Stop Shop for Outdoor Power Equipment & Fireplaces in Wellington**

At STIHL SHOP Petone, Porirua, and Upper Hutt, we’re your one-stop shop for quality outdoor power equipment, lawnmowers, servicing, and expert advice across Wellington. Whether you’re a keen home gardener, an arborist, landscaper, sports field groundsman, or civil construction professional, we have the equipment you need to get the job done.  
  
We stock quality STIHL outdoor power equipment for convenient and quiet operation. Our lithium-ion battery range provides cordless performance and freedom of movement without the need for petrol. From keeping the garden looking its best at home through to demanding commercial applications, we have reliable equipment to suit homeowners and professional operators alike.   
  
From petrol and [electric lawnmowers] to robotic and [ride-on mowers], we have a mower to suit every type of lawn and application. Our range also includes chainsaws, hedge trimmers, [line trimmers] and brushcutters, leaf blowers, pole saws, water blasters, [portable generators] and more. Whatever outdoor job you’re tackling, our experienced team can help you choose the right equipment for the job.  
  
We’re also proud to supply woodfires and [fireplaces] for homeowners looking for a reliable heating solution. Our team can help you find the right option for your home, and STIHL SHOP works with professional installers that help make the whole process easy.  
  
We’re also home to the [Marine Team], providing specialist marine sales, advice and support. Talk to our Marine Team on [0800 567 800] or explore our marine range and services online.  
  
With three convenient stores in Petone, Porirua and Upper Hutt, we make it easy to access quality outdoor power equipment, lawnmowers, woodfires, expert advice and ongoing support all in one place.
