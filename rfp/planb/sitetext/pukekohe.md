# About

**Local Experts. Premium Equipment. Outstanding Service.**  
  
 Proudly locally owned by Steve and Marlene Wright, STIHL SHOP™ Pukekohe has been serving the Franklin community since 2015.

Located in our purpose-built showroom at 16 Wrightson Way, we offer a premium range of **Outdoor Power Equipment and Home Heating**, backed by an extensive selection of accessories, safety equipment and spare parts.

Our experienced team is here to help you find the right equipment for the job, with **expert advice, professional servicing and genuine after-sales support**.

Our fully equipped service centre is staffed by skilled, industry-trained technicians, and every piece of Outdoor Power Equipment is tested before it leaves our store.

**Quality products. Expert advice. Service you can rely on.**

# Service Workshop

## Expert Servicing. Quality Repairs. Less Downtime.

Keep your outdoor power equipment performing at its best with **expert servicing and repairs from STIHL SHOP™ Pukekohe**.

Our fully equipped workshop services **STIHL and a wide range of leading outdoor power equipment brands**, including Masport, Gravely, Hustler, Cub Cadet, Hansa, Honda, Meiwa, Billy Goat, Wright, ATEX, Atom and GT Power. We can also service most leading competitor brands.

Our **industry-trained technicians and modern diagnostic equipment** allow us to accurately identify issues and complete repairs efficiently, helping save you time and money.

We also offer a comprehensive **sharpening service** for chains, hedge trimmers, secateurs, hedge clippers, lawnmower blades and chipper blades.

**From routine servicing to repairs and sharpening, you can rely on STIHL SHOP™ Pukekohe to keep your equipment working hard.**  
  
 We work hard to complete every job efficiently while maintaining the high standard of workmanship our customers expect.
