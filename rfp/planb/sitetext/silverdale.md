# ****WELCOME TO STIHL SHOP SILVERDALE****.

For over half a century Silverdale Mowers has been your locally owned outdoor power equipment retailer, providing solutions for homeowners, contractors, farmers, businesses & schools in the **North Shore** and **Rodney region**.  
  
We provide a great range of outdoor power tools, gardening equipment products plus accessories.

[VIEW PROMOTIONS]

[BOOK A SERVICE]

Our service and repair centre has fully trained technicians with spare parts stocks which makes our store a complete go-to for your needs.  
  
Our Products and Services include:

*   Lawnmowers, Ride on mowers, Generators and Water Pumps
*   Garden tools
*   Service and repair of reputable brands of outdoor power equipment
*   Protective and work wear
*   Trade accounts (upon application)
*   Expert technical advice and support

We are proud to be licensed dealers for STIHL who have set the standards in chainsaws and outdoor power equipment for 100 years.

## **Local in your community for over 30 Years**. ****We are open SEVEN days for your convenience so come on in and deal directly with the folks who actually use the gear.****

# Service

## Regular servicing of your outdoor power equipment will safeguard what you value the most in our STIHL products – longevity and performance.

We service & fix most brands & have a trained team to help you with your servicing and repair needs. With our factory-trained and certified technicians, your gear will be in the best hands possible.  
  
We offer prompt and effective service not only for your STIHL products, but for other products too using genuine parts.  
  
We have a fully equipped service workshop with the latest diagnostic tools to keep your machine running as it should be, as well as expert advice to ensure you get the best out of your equipment.  
  
We have many years expertise with Cub Cadet ride on mowers and welcome previous Gatmans customers for their servicing needs.
