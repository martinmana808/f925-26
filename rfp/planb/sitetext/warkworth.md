## Service

Here at STIHL SHOP Warkworth we like good gear and that doesn't stop at our product offering either. Our service department is equipped with state of the art tools and equipment to ensure that we are as efficient and accurate as possible when it comes to looking after your gear.

​

Whether it be our Ultra Sonic parts cleaner or the amazing Farnzen SA6 Chain Sharpener from Germnany (see video below) you know your gear is in good hands at STIHL Shop Warkworth.

​

We also service ride-on mowers and offer a pickup and delivery service (callout fee applies)if you are unable to bring your mower in, just give us a call to book a service or repair.

##### A BIT ABOUT US

Our Story

We took over ownership of STIHL SHOP Warkworth in April 2012 and immediately went about introducing a range of "best in class" specialised products to offer our customers. From flax knives to chainsaws,push mowers to ride-ons, gas grills to kamado cookers, when it comes to outdoor power equipment or outdoor cooking we've got you sorted.  
  
 We also service what we sell so you know your investment will be backed up for the useful life of the product.

So if you need help with things outdoors, pop in and see us sometime and we will do our best to assist you in getting sorted.

While you are here check out our shop mascot"Bugsby"the dragonfly (pictured right)or some of our vintage STIHL chainsaws specially imported from Germany.
