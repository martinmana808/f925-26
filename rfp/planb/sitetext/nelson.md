## STIHL SHOP Nelson

Stihl Shop Nelson is now proudly owned and operated by Isaac and Michelle Russell.  
We specialise in top quality Outdoor Power Equipment including: Stihl, Masport, Honda, Rover, Cub Cadet, Canycom and Hansa Chippers. Our friendly, efficient service is second to none and we have a diverse range of mechanical and engineering expertise to meet your needs.  
 Jason and the team have over 20 years of mechanical experience and they are ready to breathe life back into your Outdoor Power Equipment.
