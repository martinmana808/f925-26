## STIHL SHOP Hamilton East is one of Waikato's Leading outdoor power equipment retailers and service workshops.

We specialise in top quality ride on mowers and outdoor power equipment. We stock the best and most respected brands including STIHL, Walker, Masport, Cub Cadet, Honda Power Equipment and Hansa. Our staff have extensive knowledge and are ready to assist you in making the right decision when buying outdoor power equipment to meet both your requirements and your budget.

Our friendly efficient service is second to none and we have a diverse range of mechanical and engineering expertise to meet your needs.

Joel and Alex Hansen along with the team offer full retail sales & service, having skilled technicians operating in a fully equipped workshop.

## EQUIPMENT SERVICING

STIHL SHOP Hamilton East’s Service Centre

Regular ongoing care and maintenance of outdoor power equipment is critical to ensure you get top results and long-lasting life out of your equipment.

At STIHL SHOP Hamilton East we service all major brands, whether it be your small residential outdoor equipment to commercial/construction equipment.

Stihl Shop Hamilton East have the gear & over 30 years’ experience with qualified technicians to get the job done.
