# ****Welcome to STIHL SHOP™ Glenfield.****

Your North Shore experts in outdoor power equipment.  
At STIHL SHOP Glenfield, Raman and his team are here to bring you the best brands, expert advice, and reliable service — all from a bright, modern store right in the heart of Wairau Valley.  
  
Whether you’re a homeowner, contractor, or business, our goal is to match you with the right equipment for your needs.  
  
All our products are**fully assembled, fuelled, tested and ready to go**, so you can experience the quality firsthand. Our team has genuine, practical knowledge with STIHL and all major outdoor power brands, ensuring you get great service and reliable advice every time.
