Mike Supplier model & scope VQ-01–02

VQ-01MIKE-1

Supplier engagement model

Is the intention of this RFP to appoint a single, exclusive contracted supplier for the entire STIHL SHOP dealer website network, or to establish a preferred/approved supplier that individual dealers may choose to engage independently?

VQ-02MIKE-2

Network scope & adoption

Is the ultimate goal full network coverage — including the migration and replacement of all existing dealer websites (~88–92 sites) — or will adoption be optional, covering only dealers who elect to move to the new platform? If the entire network is in scope, what happens to the existing dealer websites and their content?

Why it matters: this one sizes the technical requirements more than any other.

Martin Contract, award & ownership VQ-03–05

VQ-03MARTIN-1

Contracting party & payment

Who is the vendor's client in this engagement — STIHL NZ centrally, or each dealer individually? Specifically: who signs the contract, and who pays the setup and monthly fees (head office, the dealers, or a hybrid)?

VQ-04MARTIN-2

What is actually awarded

What exactly does the winning vendor receive at the end of this process — an exclusive network-wide contract, preferred-supplier status, or a paid pilot leading to a staged rollout? What commitment sits behind the award?

VQ-05MARTIN-3

Platform ownership & IP model

Who owns the platform and its development under this engagement? Specifically: (a) does F925 retain ownership of the platform, operating it as an approved supplier licensed to use STIHL brand assets; or (b) does STIHL acquire the platform outright, with F925 engaged to implement it and manage the network as a service? Which model does STIHL prefer, who owns improvements built during the contract, and what happens to platform IP at the end of the relationship?

Leo Adoption & exit VQ-06–07

VQ-06LEONEL-1

STIHL NZ's role in driving adoption

Will STIHL NZ actively promote the appointed platform to its dealer network (co-marketing, inclusion in dealer onboarding and communications, incentives to switch), or is adoption left entirely to individual dealer discretion without central promotion?

VQ-07LEONEL-2

Contract term & exit provisions

What is the intended initial contract length and renewal structure? If the relationship is not renewed or is terminated, what is the transition process for dealers — do they retain their site and domain outright, does STIHL take over hosting, or is there a defined handover window?

🤖 Claude suggests Pilot, domains, incumbents, brand VQ-08–11

VQ-08C-1

Pilot terms & success criteria

Will the December 2026 pilot phase be paid, and is progression from pilot to network rollout contingent on defined success criteria? If so, what will be measured, over what period, and who decides?

VQ-09C-2

Domain ownership & DNS authority

Who owns and controls dealer domain names today, and who should own them going forward? For each migration, who is authorised to make DNS changes, and does STIHL prefer domains consolidated under central management?

Why it matters: at ~88 cutovers this is the single biggest operational coordination point.

VQ-10C-3

Incumbent supplier contracts

Are dealers currently under contract with their existing website suppliers, and who is expected to manage those exits — the vendor, the dealer, or STIHL? Are there notice periods or termination costs that would affect the rollout timeline?

VQ-11C-4

Brand assets & template certification

Will STIHL supply the official brand asset kit (logos, licensed typography, approved product imagery) and a formal sign-off process to certify the platform template against brand guidelines during Phase 1? And who arbitrates if a dealer's local content is disputed on compliance grounds?
