Martin’s planAs written, 18 Sep.

  1. Grab the dealer directory. Tidy it up.
  2. Feed it to Claude in multi-tenancy. Create a draft site for each dealer, using their imagery from the website scraped, and the info from their own website and our spreadsheet.
  3. Deploy each one to a free URL — if our infrastructure can’t hold it, serve static sites for this one-time demo.
  4. Create a landing page for UNIFY, better than f925.works/unify.
  5. Make a massive email list. Create good emailing.
  6. Send each dealer their draft website (weird URL) and their “allow BRANDKIT posting” link.
  7. $100 a month. No setup fee. Social BRANDKIT posting included.

Three changes, one addition, one warningThe instinct is right. These are the edits.

1 · Don’t build 90. Build 47.

The 47 with no site are the ones for whom a preview lands: nothing to compare it to, nobody to offend. The 41 with sites paid someone; a draft that replaces their current site reads as an insult to whoever built it, and several are multi-brand shops where our template is wrong anyway. They get a different, later message — “your site will need to meet STIHL SHOP standards; here is the easy way” — and no draft.

2 · No scraping, no static sites. The platform already does this.

For the 47 there is no website to scrape. What we have is enough: name, address, phone, email, owner names, town, region (the sheet) + Google Places (hours, photos, rating, review count) + their stihlshop.co.nz store page. Those are exactly the wizard’s fields. Generate one profile per dealer, load 47 tenants into UNIFY flagged preview, and serve them from the existing dealer-site app on a wildcard: <town>.preview.f925.works. One DNS record, one app, no second hosting stack. noindex, no login, a banner: “Preview · not live · Make this mine →”.

3 · $100/month is right for a farmer. Price it as an intro, not a list price.

“$100 a month for the first 12 months, no setup, cancel any time, social posting included” is a yes. “$100 forever” undercuts the RFP proposal at $600 and becomes unexplainable if STIHL later asks why the preferred channel costs six times more. Intro now, standard price after year one; the intro cohort becomes the pilot cohort if the RFP lands. This is the farmer tier the rant asked for.

The addition · this is Plan A’s engine too

Same 47 previews, same email, sent with “recommended by STIHL NZ” if we win and without it if we don’t. Sections 2.2–2.4 of PREFERRED describe exactly this. And walking into the presentation on 22 October with “we have already built a site for every STIHL SHOP dealer that doesn’t have one — here are 47” is the most convincing thing anyone in that room will see. Build it once.

The warning · timing and manners

47 unsolicited “here is your website” emails to STIHL dealers, three weeks before STIHL picks its preferred vendor, reach Mark within a day. Allowed under the model he described — but it looks like going around the process while inside it, and the evaluation is 10% cost, 90% judgement. Build now, send after 30 October — as the preferred partner, or as Little Rocket would. Exception: Kumeu, Albany, Te Awamutu are warm and asked; they get theirs this week.

  2. Enrich the 47 (Claude, 1 day). Google Places: hours, photos, rating, reviews, lat/lng. stihlshop.co.nz store page: anything extra. Store per dealer.
