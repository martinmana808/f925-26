01

Executive summary

Section 19 of your RFP describes the desired outcome: a nationally consistent, professionally managed dealer website ecosystem that strengthens the STIHL SHOP brand, complements stihlshop.co.nz, provides dealers with local flexibility, delivers a superior customer experience, requires minimal dealer administration, and supports future growth of both STIHL and approved supplier brands.

We did not read that paragraph as a wish list. We read it as a description of a platform we have already built, deployed, and operated inside the STIHL SHOP network. UNIFY runs the live dealer website of STIHL SHOP Tauranga today — one shared codebase, centrally governed, locally personalised, with every STIHL purchase journey handed to stihlshop.co.nz with the dealer's attribution intact.

  • Nationally consistent — every dealer site is rendered by one template. Brand standards are not a guideline dealers may ignore; they are code dealers cannot touch.
  • Complements stihlshop.co.nz — dealer sites cannot transact. Every STIHL product action deep-links to the dealer's own page on the national store, preserving sales attribution. This is engineered into the platform, not policed by policy.
  • Local flexibility — dealers personalise 20+ approved content areas (story, team, services, hours, local brands, community) through a secure two-factor portal, with every change reviewed before it goes live.
  • Superior customer experience — the live pilot measurably outperforms every dealer site in the network today, on speed, stability, and mobile experience (Appendix A).
  • Minimal dealer administration — our founding premise: unlimited change requests, handled by us, published within 48 hours. Most dealers never touch a CMS at all.
  • Supports future growth — a configurable approved-supplier module (Honda, Masport, Cub Cadet, Hansa, Gravely live today), plus a roadmap of workshop booking, analytics, and campaign tooling that one deploy delivers to the entire network at once.

Why this bid is different

Your timeline asks for a pilot store deployment in December 2026. Our pilot has been live since early 2026. You can open it right now, run any performance tool against it, click every product journey, and speak with the dealer who runs their business on it. We are not proposing to build this platform — we are proposing to roll out the one that already works, to the rest of the network, on your timetable.

For the last 18 months, F925's core focus has been UNIFY: a multi-tenant platform purpose-built for the STIHL SHOP dealer network. 05

Proposed solution

UNIFY is a managed dealer-website framework: one platform, centrally operated by F925, serving every participating dealer a fast, brand-locked, locally personalised website — with a secure portal for approved dealer updates and a managed content service that takes website administration off dealers' desks entirely.

5.1 · Platform architecture

One codebase, every dealer

Dealer sites are rendered by a single shared template (SvelteKit, server-rendered). A visitor's dealer is resolved from the domain; the template pulls that dealer's approved content from the central platform. Design, navigation, typography, and product presentation are identical everywhere — because they are literally the same code.

Central content platform

All dealer content lives in one PostgreSQL-backed platform with a structured schema per dealer: store details, hours, departments, team, services, supplier brands, in-store products, imagery, promotions. The platform is the single source of truth; the sites are pure renderings of it.

Edge delivery

Sites are served through Cloudflare's global edge (Auckland POPs for NZ visitors) with aggressive asset caching, modern image formats (AVIF/WebP) generated automatically, and HTTP/2+3. Origin compute runs on DigitalOcean's managed App Platform.

Network-scale operations

Because there is one codebase and one platform, a seasonal campaign, a legal-text change, or a security patch deploys to all dealer sites in one action. Operating cost does not multiply with dealer count — which is what makes the flat monthly fee in Section 9 possible.

5.3 · STIHL product rules — your Sections 6 & 7

Your hard requirement — dealer websites must not facilitate the direct online sale of STIHL products, and purchase journeys must redirect to the relevant page on www.stihlshop.co.nz — is the single most load-bearing rule in this RFP, and it is the one we would ask you to test hardest in evaluation:

  • No transaction path exists. The platform contains no cart, no checkout, no payment integration. The rule cannot be broken by a dealer, an editor, or an accident.
  • Attribution-preserving deep links. Every STIHL product CTA — menus, category tiles, article product cards, promotions — resolves to the dealer's own namespace on the national store, so the visit lands as that dealer's customer. We follow your recommended pattern (link out at category/product level rather than maintaining a local catalogue) as our default architecture.
  • Product data stays current by construction. Because dealer sites present STIHL categories and link to stihlshop.co.nz for detail, pricing and specifications are always the national site's own — there is no locally duplicated catalogue to go stale.   • Approved supplier brands, governed. Dealers may promote and sell non-STIHL brands relevant to their business through a configurable supplier module (Honda, Masport, Cub Cadet, Hansa, and Gravely live on the pilot), with brand pages and in-store product listings that route to enquiry journeys — all subject to the same central review as every other content area.

5.5 · Content management — your Section 9

The managed service. F925 is responsible for all website content updates, national promotion and campaign deployment, seasonal campaigns across all dealer sites, image and asset management, SEO optimisation, brand-compliance monitoring and reporting, and network-wide changes — exactly the supplier responsibility list in your Section 9. Our model was designed around your stated goal: minimise the time dealers spend maintaining their websites. Dealers get unlimited change requests with a 48-hour publication guarantee; most never touch a CMS at all.

The dealer portal. For dealers who want hands-on control of their local content, UNIFY provides a secure content-management portal:

  • Two-factor authentication on every account; per-dealership user accounts with roles.
  • Approved local updates across every dealer-owned area: store information, trading hours, contact details, staff profiles, local promotions and events, community sponsorships, store-specific product ranges.
  • Governed self-service: every submission is staged, reviewed field-by-field centrally, and published — typically same-day, always within 48 hours. Low-risk facts (trading hours, phone numbers) publish immediately with post-publication review. This is how "approved local updates" stays true at 88 dealers: brand integrity is enforced by the workflow, not by hoping dealers read the guidelines.
  • Built-in messaging between dealer and our team on every request, with full history.

Training and support. A guided ten-step onboarding wizard collects each dealer's content at rollout; an interactive, no-login training sandbox lets any dealer staff member practise the portal risk-free; and our service desk (Section 7) backs both. User permissions, training, and support — your Section 9 closing requirement — are platform features, not a PDF.

The design brief in your words — "the design should feel like an extension of the national website" — is validated on the live pilot, where customers move between the dealer site and their dealer's pages on stihlshop.co.nz without a visible seam. During Phase 1 we will formally certify the template against your brand guidelines and encode any refinements once — after which every dealer site inherits them permanently.

Legal & compliance

10.1 · Ownership — your Section 18

We affirm without qualification that STIHL SHOP retains ownership of:

  • Branding — all STIHL SHOP marks, design assets, and brand expressions remain STIHL SHOP property; our template is a licensed implementation of them.
  • Customer data — every lead, form submission, and analytics record is captured into infrastructure we operate on your behalf, stored in New Zealand-governed cloud regions, and exportable to STIHL SHOP at any time in standard formats. Analytics properties are structured for transfer to a STIHL SHOP-owned organisation.
  • Dealer content — all dealer-supplied text, imagery, and structured content is exportable per dealer on request and on exit.
  • Digital assets — imagery and media in the platform's asset store belong to STIHL SHOP and its dealers, never to F925.

A data-ownership and exit schedule formalising all of the above — including handover obligations and timelines — is offered as a contract appendix.

10.2 · Regulatory compliance

  • New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 — every dealer site carries its own privacy policy; personal information is collected only for stated purposes, consent-gated analytics, and no data is sold or shared beyond the service.
  • Fair Trading Act — no misleading representations anywhere in the platform; where our optional AI shop assistant is deployed, it is disclosed as an AI assistant and guard-railed against quoting prices or specifications.
  • Consumer Guarantees Act — dealer sites carry no transactional claims; all product sales and their guarantees run through the national platform's established terms.
  • Cybersecurity — controls aligned to the NZ Information Security Manual's guidance proportionate to this workload: encryption in transit and at rest, two-factor authentication on all administrative access, least-privilege access, dependency scanning, WAF, monitored infrastructure, and rehearsed recovery.
